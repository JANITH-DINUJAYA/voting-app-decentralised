VoteChain Governance Hub

A fully auditable, cryptographically-secure, identity-verified blockchain elections platform. Every vote is signed, hashed, and immutably recorded on the ledger.

1. Identity Verification

Generate a cryptographic wallet and submit your National Identity Card (NIC). An on-chain verifier audits your submission.

2. On-Chain Audit

The system verifier node reviews your NIC image and approves your identity. The result is mined to the ledger.

3. Cast Signed Ballot

Sign your vote with your private key. The cryptographic signature and transaction hash are permanently stored on-chain.
